   - **Source fidelity**: compare assets added or changed in this edit with their recorded sources. Check sanitization, file preservation, and placement against `./sdm-design.md`; allow only user-requested visual changes. Disclose missing authentic assets; never fabricate replacements.
   - **Existing assets**: for untouched assets in existing or imported decks, preserve the current deck files. Missing historic provenance must not trigger re-sourcing, replacement, or block unrelated edits. Do not claim unverified branding is verified.
   - **Brand colors**: compare added or changed theme and widget colors with their sources, respecting the opaque-color limitation. `theme-drift` checks consistency, not source accuracy.
   - **Motifs**: every decorative shape belongs to one of the deck's declared motifs and has a job -- framing content or an image, dividing, anchoring, or bleeding off an edge (`./sdm-design.md`). Delete floaters: a shape in open space or on a photo with no job is a defect, and clean whitespace beats filler. A shape-drawn chart is the same defect at any size, and so is a shape diagram past a small labeled flow of three to five boxes -- replace those with a widget, not a generated image. A shape collage imitating pictorial content becomes a generated image, and the small labeled flow itself stays as shapes (`./sdm-design.md`).
   - **Type scale**: at most 2 families and 3 weights deck-wide; body text stays 19pt+ and serif is used selectively. Titles are consistent per header style -- every slide using the same style shares one `sizePt` -- and a hero or statement title is its own deliberate style, not a one-off enlargement of the content title. Large titles are welcome when they are the visual idea; otherwise confirm they do not crowd or compete with the slide's image, stat, chart, or body.
   - **Grid**: content slides share the deck margin and content-top line, and every slide that uses a header style reuses that style's exact title frame and `sizePt`; elements meant to align share exact `x` or `y` values.
   - **Craft**: check for the avoidable failure modes in `## Designed, not templated` (`./sdm-design.md`): text plus rules where a meaningful visual would help, mechanically equal spacing or repeated stripes, mismatched photography, generic vector art where a photo fits, or related multiline/list copy mechanically split into one-line elements without an independent layout, style, action, or motion purpose. Sparse and typography-only slides are fine when intentional.
   - **Contrast**: every text-over-image element has a scrim shape between the image and the text in paint order.
   - **Message**: each slide makes one point a reader could state in a sentence; content titles hold the register chosen in planning (assertion or topic) and, read in order on their own, carry the storyline -- the cover, agenda, and section dividers name structure and sit outside that check, and the register may change once after an appendix or reference divider; every chart states its takeaway on the slide; where the deck seeks a decision, the ask is on a slide (`<communication>` in `./sdm-principles.md`, and `./professional.md` when the Professional theme was selected).
   - **Density and copy**: text density matches the delivery context named in planning -- a presented slide holds a title plus a few short lines or one visual with a caption; a read-ahead slide may be fuller -- with body text at 19pt+ and nothing shrunk to fit; no emoji anywhere; none of the machine-written vocabulary listed in `<communication>`.
